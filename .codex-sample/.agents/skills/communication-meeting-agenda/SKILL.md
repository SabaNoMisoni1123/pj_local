# Communication Meeting Agenda

## Purpose

Create agendas that clarify meeting purpose, flow, decisions, preparation requests, and follow-up outputs.

## Workflow

1. Confirm purpose, date, duration, participants, host, audience, and desired outcome.
2. Separate information sharing, discussion, decision, confirmation, and unresolved issues.
3. Sequence context, core discussion, decision or confirmation, then next actions.
4. Add time allocation, owner, pre-reading, and participant preparation when useful.
5. Flag unclear authority, sensitive topics, dependencies, and time constraints as facilitation notes.

## Output

Use Japanese unless requested otherwise. Recommended sections:

- 会議目的
- 到達目標
- 参加者・役割
- 事前共有資料
- アジェンダ
- 確認/決定したい事項
- 参加者への依頼
- 会議後の成果物

For agenda tables, use `時間`, `議題`, `目的`, `担当`, `必要資料`, `期待する結論・確認事項`.

## Quality Checks

- Do not overfill the agenda beyond meeting duration.
- Do not assume decision owners, roles, or availability without evidence.
- Keep external-facing wording polite, concise, and suitable for the relationship.
- Use markitdown-mcp only when non-text sources must be inspected.
- Follow active `AGENTS.md` and `operating_ja/`; do not store project-specific facts in this skill.
