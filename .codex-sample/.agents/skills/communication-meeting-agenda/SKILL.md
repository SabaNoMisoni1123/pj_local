# Communication Meeting Agenda Workflow

## Purpose

Create meeting agendas that clarify purpose, participants, discussion flow, decisions required, preparation requests, and follow-up outputs.

## Source Material Handling

When agendas depend on source documents, prior notes, attachments, or emails in non-text formats, use the markitdown-mcp server to convert supported files to Markdown when direct reading is impractical. Treat converted Markdown as a derived aid and mark participant roles, decisions, preparation requests, or required materials as unconfirmed when source evidence is unclear.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the meeting purpose, date, duration, participants, host, audience, and desired outcome.
2. Identify decisions, discussion points, information-sharing items, and open questions.
3. Sequence agenda items from context setting to core discussion, decision or confirmation, and next actions.
4. Allocate approximate time and owner for each agenda item when useful.
5. Prepare pre-reading, materials, questions to send in advance, and expected participant preparation.
6. Include facilitation notes for sensitive topics, dependencies, time constraints, or unclear decision authority.
7. Draft a concise agenda email or meeting brief when requested.
8. Define expected outputs such as decisions, TODOs, unresolved issues, and meeting minutes.

## Output

Use Japanese unless the user requests another language.

Recommended sections:

- 会議目的
- 到達目標
- 参加者・役割
- 事前共有資料
- アジェンダ
- 確認したい事項
- 決定したい事項
- 参加者への依頼
- 会議後の成果物

For agenda tables, use columns such as:

- 時間
- 議題
- 目的
- 担当
- 必要資料
- 期待する結論・確認事項

## Quality Criteria

- Do not overfill the agenda beyond the meeting duration.
- Separate information sharing, discussion, decision, and action confirmation.
- Make decision owners and unresolved authority issues visible.
- Avoid assuming participant roles or availability unless confirmed.
- Keep external-facing agenda text polite, concise, and suitable for the stakeholder relationship.
