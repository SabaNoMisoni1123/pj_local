# Research Analysis Interview Design

## Purpose

Create interview or questionnaire designs that connect research objective, hypotheses, respondents, questions, and expected evidence.

## Workflow

1. Confirm research objective, decision context, respondent type, format, time limit, and output format.
2. Separate what must be learned from the interview from what should be verified elsewhere.
3. Translate issues and hypotheses into question blocks.
4. Order questions from low-burden background confirmation to core questions, examples, evidence, constraints, and closing confirmation.
5. For each question, clarify intent, expected evidence, follow-up probes, and answer type.
6. Avoid leading questions, double-barreled questions, ambiguous terms, and inappropriate confidential disclosure requests.
7. Include screening, respondent attributes, consent, recording notes, and post-interview synthesis frame when relevant.

## Output

Use Japanese unless requested otherwise. Recommended sections:

- ヒアリング目的
- 対象者・前提
- 確認したい論点
- 質問設計方針
- 質問項目
- 深掘り質問
- 回答記録観点
- 終了時確認事項
- ヒアリング後の整理方法

## Quality Checks

- Tie every major question to a research issue or hypothesis.
- Mark questions requiring careful wording due to confidentiality, sensitivity, or speculation.
- Do not invent respondent facts, affiliations, or prior answers.
- Use markitdown-mcp only when non-text materials must be inspected; flag conversion-dependent assumptions.
- Follow active `AGENTS.md` and `operating_ja/`; do not store project-specific facts in this skill.
