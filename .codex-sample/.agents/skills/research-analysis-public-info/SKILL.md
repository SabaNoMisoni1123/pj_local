# Research Analysis Public Information

## Purpose

Create research outputs that separate sourced facts, interpretation, unknowns, and confirmation items.

## Workflow

1. Confirm research objective, scope, geography, time period, and output format.
2. Identify source priority and whether web research is allowed or required.
3. Browse or verify latest/current information, changeable facts, or user-specified external sources.
4. Prefer primary sources, official publications, original statistics, laws, standards, and source documents.
5. Record source owner, publication date or access date, URL or path, and the supported claim.
6. Separate facts, interpretation, assumptions, unknowns, and confirmation items.
7. Do not use files under `old/`, `_old/`, or similar obsolete-information directories at all. Do not use generated, draft, converted, or low-confidence sources as sole evidence.
8. Report limitations, gaps, conflicting evidence, and recommended next checks.

## Output

Use Japanese unless requested otherwise. Recommended sections:

- 調査目的
- 要約
- 調査結果
- 比較表または論点整理
- 根拠
- 不明点・要確認
- 調査上の制約

## Quality Checks

- Do not treat search snippets or generated summaries as primary evidence.
- Link evidence to the specific claim it supports.
- Check date, version, and scope of public information.
- Use markitdown-mcp only when non-text source documents must be inspected; cite originals.
- Follow active `AGENTS.md` and `operating_ja/`; do not store project-specific facts in this skill.
